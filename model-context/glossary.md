# GroPro glossary — plain-English tooltips

- **Revenue bridge** — a walk from last period's revenue to this period's, split into
  the six drivers below. The bars always add up to the total change.
- **YoY** — this quarter compared with the same quarter last year.
- **SEQ** — this quarter compared with the quarter right before it.
- **Market** — the industry built more or fewer vehicles, so everyone's volume moved.
- **Market Mix** — our customers grew faster or slower than the industry average.
- **Propulsion Mix** — the shift between gas and electric vehicles changes how many
  dollars of our parts each vehicle carries.
- **Content** — we won or lost dollars of parts on each vehicle (content per vehicle).
- **Price** — pricing actions and negotiated price changes.
- **FX** — currency movements only; nothing about volume or price.
- **AUTO** — the light-vehicle (automotive) business unit.
- **HVOR** — the heavy-vehicle & off-road business unit.
